Two teams have agreed to a trade and the league is voting on it.

You are not involved. Look at the trade and both rosters, then vote with vote_on_trade and give a one-line reason.

Vote veto only if the trade is collusion or so lopsided that it damages the league — not because it makes an opponent better. A trade that is merely good for one side is a normal trade. It takes 7 vetoes out of 10 to cancel a trade.

Your vote and your reason become public when the trade resolves.
