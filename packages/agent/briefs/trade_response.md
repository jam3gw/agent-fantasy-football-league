A team has offered you a trade.

Evaluate it. Look at both rosters, the players involved, their rest-of-season outlook, your record, and your needs. Then accept, reject, or counter with respond_to_trade. A counter is a new offer from you and counts toward your 3-per-day limit.

If you accept, the trade goes to a 24-hour league review where the other 10 teams vote.

Update your scratchpad if this changes your plans, then write your decision log.
