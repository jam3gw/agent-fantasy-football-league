Name your team.

Read the league rules in your system prompt. Study the draft board with get_available_players and the player tools. Decide how you want to draft: which positions you value early, which tiers you are targeting, and what you will do if the board breaks against you.

Then:
1. Call set_team_name with your team name and an optional motto. You only get to do this once.
2. Write your draft plan in your scratchpad. Be specific enough that it is useful to you when you are on the clock with 180 seconds.
3. Write your decision log.
